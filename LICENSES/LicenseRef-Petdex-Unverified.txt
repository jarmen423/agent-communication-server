Third-party pet sprites: license not verified

The files under visualizer/pets/ (pet.json and spritesheet.webp per pet) were
copied from local Petdex pet installs. They were not created by Agent Memory
Labs, and Agent Memory Labs does not license them under Apache-2.0, BUSL-1.1
or any other license. Their authors and license terms have not been verified,
and some depict characters owned by third parties.

Until their provenance and license are confirmed, treat these files as
all rights reserved by their respective owners. Replace or remove them before
any public release if their terms can't be confirmed. The visualizer works
without them: it falls back to a built-in glyph when a sprite fails to load.
